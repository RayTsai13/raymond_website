## Why

Epigraphy databases are wonderful but hard to search. Abbreviations like `IMP · CAES` break naive keyword search. Scriptorium expands common abbreviations at index time, so a search for *imperator* finds all of them.

## How

1. Normalize the transcriptions: expand abbreviations, strip editorial brackets, and fold letter variants such as V/U.
2. Store them in PostgreSQL with a `tsvector` column and trigram indexes for fuzzy matches.
3. Serve faceted search through FastAPI, with a small React front end.

## Results

Two seminars used it for assignments. The most common feedback was a request for a map view, which is next.
