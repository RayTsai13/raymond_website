## Overview

Built for a distributed systems course project: a sidecar proxy that sits next to each service and handles the unglamorous parts of networking, such as retries with jittered backoff, circuit breaking, and mutual TLS.

## Highlights

- A zero-allocation hot path for request forwarding
- A circuit breaker driven by a sliding-window error rate
- A chaos mode that injects latency and failures for testing
